# Problem 493: Under the Rainbow 
## Task
70 coloured balls are placed in an urn, 10 for each of the seven rainbow colours.

What is the expected number of distinct colours in 20 randomly picked balls?

Give your answer with nine digits after the decimal point (a.bcdefghij).

## Explanation
The number of ways to choose 20 balls from the total of 70 is $70C20$.

If a color is missing, say, **[red]**, then all of the selected balls must come from the other 60. The number of ways to choose 20 balls from the total of 60 is $60C20$. This count may include selections in which other colors are missing as well, but we are considering only whether **[red]** is absent, independently of the presence or absence of the other colors.

Therefore, the probability of a color missing is: 

$$P = \frac{60C20}{70C20}$$

The probability of a color appears is the complementary event of $P$, which is: 

$$\overline{P} = 1 - \frac{60C20}{70C20}$$

Since there are 7 distinct colors, the expected distinct colors that appear would be:

```math
EX = 7 \times (1 - \frac{60C20}{70C20}) = 6.818741802
```
